# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview
End-to-end analytics project analyzing 541,909 transactions 
from a UK-based online retailer to uncover sales trends, 
customer behavior and product performance.

## Business Questions
- What is the total revenue and order trends?
- Which countries generate the most revenue?
- Who are the top 10 customers by revenue?
- Which products drive the most sales?
- What day of the week has highest revenue?

## Tools Used
| Tool | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Python (Pandas) | Data cleaning & EDA |
| SQL (SQLite) | Data storage & querying |
| Matplotlib/Seaborn | Exploratory charts |
| Power BI | Final dashboard |

## Key Insights
- Total revenue: $8.91M
- UK drives 82% of total revenue
- Thursday is the peak revenue day
- Top customer generated $280K revenue
- PAPER CRAFT is the #1 product


## Dataset
👉 [Kaggle - E-Commerce Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/carrie1/ecommerce-data)
